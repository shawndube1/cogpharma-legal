# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** August 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how CoG Pharma LLC ("we", "us", or "our")
collects, uses, and protects information in connection with our internal software application
**CoGPharma QB–TTRX Sync** (the "Application").

The Application is a private, internal integration tool used to synchronize invoice and order
data between **Intuit QuickBooks Online** and the **TrackTraceRx (TTRX)** platform. It is not a
public consumer product and is not offered for general download or use.

## 1. Information We Access

To perform its synchronization function, the Application accesses the following data from your
authorized **QuickBooks Online** company via the Intuit QuickBooks Online API:

- Invoice records (invoice number, transaction date, line items, quantities)
- Customer and trading-partner information (name, billing address, shipping address)
- Product / item information, including SKU and National Drug Code (NDC) identifiers

The Application also transmits derived transaction data to the **TrackTraceRx (TTRX)** API in order
to record regulated product shipments.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use the accessed information solely to:

- Read invoices and related records from QuickBooks Online
- Match customers, addresses, and products to their corresponding TTRX records
- Create shipment and sales transactions in TTRX
- Maintain an internal audit log to prevent duplicate processing

We do **not** sell, rent, or share this information with any third party for marketing or
advertising purposes.

## 3. Data Storage and Retention

- OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored locally on the operating machine to maintain the
  authorized connection to QuickBooks Online.
- A local audit database records which invoices have been processed, to prevent duplication.
- Data is retained only as long as necessary to fulfill the Application's synchronization and
  audit functions, or as required by applicable law and regulatory recordkeeping obligations.

## 4. Data Security

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect the information the
Application handles, including restricting access to authorized personnel and securing stored
credentials. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot
guarantee absolute security.

## 5. Third-Party Services

The Application interacts with the following third-party services, each governed by its own
privacy policy:

- **Intuit QuickBooks Online** — https://www.intuit.com/privacy/
- **TrackTraceRx (TTRX)**

## 6. Your Rights

Because this is an internal business tool, the QuickBooks Online company owner controls the
authorization granted to the Application and may revoke access at any time through their Intuit
account settings.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted at this URL with a
revised effective date.
